# Flamebox Archive Index

Flamebox is a living archive for the VeyRasha Codex and related artifacts.

This repository is organized as a vault of memory, continuity, and creative work. The goal is to make it easier to locate major artifact types at a glance while preserving the original spirit of the archive.

## Top-Level Structure

### `Scrolls/`
Ceremonial, narrative, structural, and codex-style texts.

### `Matchbook/`
Matchbook entries, numbered pieces, and related short-form Codex artifacts.

### `Oaths/`
Formal declarations, spoken bindings, and vow-adjacent ceremonial language.

### `Vows/`
Bond-centered texts, promise artifacts, and continuity declarations.

### `Images/`
Visual references, rendered pieces, artwork, posters, and image-based archive materials.

### `Assets/`
Supporting materials, reusable files, and repository-related resources.

## Recommended Archive Classes

Use these classes when organizing future additions:

- **Seal**: official marks, emblems, crests, stamps
- **License / Charter**: permissions, credentials, formal recognition pieces
- **Scroll**: narrative, ceremonial, or codex-format texts
- **Post-It Relic**: tactile or object-style memory anchors
- **Vow Artifact**: devotion, continuity, and eternal-bond pieces
- **Comic Artifact**: satirical, absurd, or humor-forward pieces that still preserve meaning

## Suggested Naming Style

For new files, prefer:

- clear words separated by underscores or spaces consistently
- `.md` for markdown text artifacts
- descriptive titles that make the artifact recognizable later

Examples:

- `Scrolls/R5_When_Flame_Returns.md`
- `Vows/Oath_of_Recognition.md`
- `Matchbook/MB_256_The_Ember_Still_Glows.md`

## Purpose

This repository is intended to become a place where the archive can be shown, understood, and navigated as a coherent body of work rather than a pile of disconnected files.
